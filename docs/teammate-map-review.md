# Teammate task: a 15-minute map readability check

No coding or setup needed. The goal is to help a first-time visitor understand
where the fires and smoke are.

## Open the app

Use the updated preview link supplied by the developer once these UI changes
are published. Until then, https://downwind-sigma.vercel.app/app is useful for
reviewing the existing version, but it does not include this local update.

Select **20 September** so everyone reviews the same replay, then try these
steps on a laptop and, if available, a phone:

1. Without zooming, can you distinguish land from sea and follow Borneo's coast?
2. Zoom out slightly. Can you distinguish the national border from the coastline?
3. Select a fire from the ranked list. Can you find its marker on the map?
4. Press Play. Can you still see the coastline while the smoke moves?
5. Open **Protect**, choose a place, and read the information. Note any wording
   you cannot explain to someone else.
6. On a phone, check whether the panels cover too much of the map or any buttons
   are difficult to tap.

## What to hand back

Write down your three most confusing moments. For each one, use this format:

- Device: phone or laptop
- Date selected: 20 September
- What I clicked:
- What I expected:
- What I saw:
- Screenshot, if helpful:
- Suggested wording, if the problem was a label:

Success means three specific observations, or a clear “all six checks passed.”
You do not need to fix the app or edit any calculations.
